About Us & Vision

About ChestSimulator.com

ChestSimulator.com is a free, fan-made interactive utility designed for League of Legends enthusiasts. Our mission is to allow players to experience Hextech Crafting drop rates, chest opening simulations, and Baron Nashor clicker combat completely risk-free without spending real money. All character and skin assets are owned by Riot Games.

🎮 100% Free & Risk-Free

Summoners can open Hextech chests, test real skin shard probability distributions, and explore crafting mechanics without spending any Riot Points (RP) or real currency.

⚔️ Baron Nashor & Master Yi

Battle the legendary Baron Nashor using Master Yi's Alpha Strike ability. Deal high DPS, trigger critical strikes, level up your summoner rank, and collect Hextech keys.

💎 Official Data Dragon Assets

All skin splash artworks, champion portraits, tier banners, and item icons are dynamically fetched from Riot Games' official high-resolution Data Dragon CDN.

🔒 Privacy & Local Storage

Your inventory, unlocked skins, and clicker progression are stored securely in your browser's LocalStorage. No personal data is tracked, collected, or sold.
