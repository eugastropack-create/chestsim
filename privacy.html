Transparency & Security

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At ChestSimulator.com, accessible from https://chestsimulator.com (and associated hosting domains), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by ChestSimulator.com and how we use it.

1. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

Google is one of the third-party vendors on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to ChestSimulator.com and other sites on the internet.

Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on ChestSimulator.com. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see.

2. LocalStorage & Browser Game Data

ChestSimulator.com is an interactive web-based simulator. All game progression data—including unlocked champion skins, Orange Essence, Mythic Essence, Master Yi clicker levels, keys, and inventory items—is stored exclusively on your device within your web browser's HTML5 LocalStorage.

We do not transmit your game inventory, clicker progress, or simulator statistics to any external server or database. You have full control over this data, and you can reset or clear it at any time by clearing your browser cache and local storage.

3. Google Analytics & Log Files

ChestSimulator.com follows a standard procedure of using log files and Google Analytics (Measurement ID: G-J523ZKWNTY). The information collected by Google Analytics includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.

These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information to improve user experience.

4. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), among other rights, California consumers have the right to:

  • Request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data that a business has collected about consumers.
  • Request that a business delete any personal data about the consumer that a business has collected.
  • Request that a business that sells a consumer's personal data, not sell the consumer's personal data (We DO NOT sell personal information).

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at contact@chestsimulator.com.

5. GDPR Data Protection Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following:

  • The right to access – You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification – You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure – You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing – You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.

6. Children's Information Protection

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. ChestSimulator.com does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

7. Contact Information

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact our compliance team via email at: contact@chestsimulator.com.
