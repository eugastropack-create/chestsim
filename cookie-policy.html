Cookies & Storage

Cookie Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work properly, provide analytics, and deliver personalized advertisements through ad networks like Google AdSense.

2. How We Use Cookies & Storage

ChestSimulator.com utilizes cookies and browser LocalStorage in the following ways:

  • Essential Functional Storage: Saves your language preference (EN/TR), volume levels, theme color tone, and game inventory (skins, essence, clicker level) locally in your browser.
  • Google Analytics: Helps us understand how visitors interact with our guides and simulator so we can optimize performance.
  • Google AdSense Advertising Cookies: Google uses cookies (including DoubleClick DART cookies) to serve relevant ads based on prior visits to our website or other sites.

3. Managing & Disabling Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). To opt out of personalized advertising by Google, visit Google Ad Settings or AboutAds Choices.
