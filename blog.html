Mythic Essence (ME) & Prestige 2.0 Skin Shop Guide

📅 Economy Guide ⏱️ 5 min read 💎 Mythic Mastery

Optimization & Math

Orange Essence Economy: Disenchant vs. Re-roll 3 Skin Shards

📅 Strategy Guide ⏱️ 5 min read 📊 Mathematical Analysis

A common dilemma for League of Legends summoners is whether to disenchant low-tier skin shards for Orange Essence (OE) or reroll 3 skin shards into a random unowned permanent skin.

The Math: When to Disenchant vs. When to Reroll

Disenchanting gives exactly 20% of the skin's RP value in Orange Essence (e.g., 520 RP skin = 104 OE, 1350 RP skin = 270 OE). To upgrade an Epic 1350 RP skin shard to a permanent, you need 1050 OE.

🎯 Golden Rule of Crafting:
  • Reroll: Always reroll 520 RP and 750 RP skin shards! Their disenchant value is too low (~100 OE), whereas rerolling gives you an equal chance at 1350 RP or 1820 RP permanent skins.
  • Disenchant: Disenchant unwanted 1820 RP (Legendary) and 1350 RP (Epic) duplicate shards to bank Orange Essence for the skins you genuinely play.
